# Cold Brewing Stand

The Cold Brewing Stand is a Nether-free alternative to the vanilla Brewing Stand, perfect for players who want to explore alchemy without venturing into dangerous dimensions.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:cold_brewing_stand" />

## How It Works

The Cold Brewing Stand functions identically to a vanilla Brewing Stand, with one key difference: it uses **Snowballs** as fuel instead of Blaze Powder!

This makes it ideal for:
- Skyblock or peaceful playthroughs
- Players who haven't reached the Nether yet
- Cold biome bases where snowballs are plentiful

## Frozen Petals Brewing

**Frozen Petals** can be used as a brewing ingredient to create **Awkward Potions** from Water Bottles, just like Nether Wart. This provides a complete Nether-free path to potion brewing!

To brew:
1. Fill the Cold Brewing Stand with snowballs (up to 20)
2. Place Water Bottles in the bottom slots
3. Add Frozen Petals to the ingredient slot
4. Wait for brewing to complete - you'll have Awkward Potions!

From there, you can continue brewing other potions using standard ingredients.
