# Shimmersteel Ingot

Shimmersteel is the magical metal at the heart of Aurorabound. Infused with aurora energy, it possesses unique properties that make it invaluable for crafting enhanced tools and equipment.

## Obtaining Shimmersteel

Creating Shimmersteel Ingots is a two-step process:

### Step 1: Craft Unrefined Shimmersteel

First, craft Unrefined Shimmersteel from Iron Ingots and Aurora Shards:

<Recipe id="auroral:unrefined_shimmersteel" />

### Step 2: Glacial Basin Infusion

Infuse the Unrefined Shimmersteel in a [Glacial Basin](glacial_basin.md) filled with Liquid Aura:

1. Place a Glacial Basin under open sky in a cold biome
2. Wait for an [Aurora Event](../aurora/aurora_events.md) to fill the basin completely
3. Right-click the basin with Unrefined Shimmersteel
4. The basin consumes **all** its Liquid Aura to create a Shimmersteel Ingot

**Important:** The basin must be completely full (3/3 aura) to perform this infusion.

## Properties

- **High Enchantability** - Shimmersteel items accept enchantments easily
- **Aurora Affinity** - Tools and armor repair themselves during aurora events
- **Slightly Better than Iron** - Base stats exceed iron but below diamond

## Uses

Shimmersteel Ingots are used to upgrade iron equipment via the Smithing Table:

- <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_pickaxe" scale="0.75" /> [Shimmersteel Tools](tools.md) - Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel, Hoe, Sword
- <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_bow" scale="0.75" /> [Shimmersteel Bow](bow.md) - Fires Star-Shots using snowballs
- <ItemImage id="shimmer_spear" scale="0.75" /> [Shimmer Spear](spear.md) - Long-reach piercing spear

You'll need a **Shimmersteel Upgrade Smithing Template** to perform these upgrades.
