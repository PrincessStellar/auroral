# Shimmersteel Armor Trims

Shimmersteel Ingots can be used as an armor trim material, allowing you to customize any armor with an icy blue accent!

## Using Shimmersteel Trims

1. Obtain a **Smithing Template** (any armor trim pattern from vanilla structures)
2. Place the template, your armor piece, and a **Shimmersteel Ingot** in a Smithing Table
3. The resulting armor will have a beautiful ice-blue trim matching the aurora's glow

## Appearance

The Shimmersteel trim has a distinctive icy gradient:
- Deep blue shadows
- Cool steel-blue midtones
- Bright ice-white highlights

The effect is reminiscent of the Aurora itself, with colors that shimmer between blue and silver.

## Compatibility

Shimmersteel trims work with:
- All vanilla armor types (leather, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond, netherite)
- Most modded armor that supports the vanilla trim system
- All vanilla trim patterns (Coast, Dune, Eye, Host, Raiser, Rib, Sentry, Shaper, Silence, Snout, Spire, Tide, Vex, Ward, Wayfinder, Wild)

## Tips

- Shimmersteel trims pair especially well with diamond and iron armor for a cohesive cold theme
- Combine with Shimmerweave armor for the ultimate frozen adventurer look
- The high enchantability of Shimmersteel means trimmed armor still enchants beautifully
