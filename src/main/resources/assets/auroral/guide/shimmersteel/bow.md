# The Shimmersteel Bow

The Shimmersteel Bow is a unique ranged weapon that transforms ordinary snowballs into devastating Star-Shots.

## Star-Shots

Unlike regular bows that use arrows, the Shimmersteel Bow uses **Snowballs** as ammunition. When fired, the snowball transforms into a Star-Shot.

### Star-Shot Properties
- **Damage** - Higher base damage than arrows
- **Flashbang Effect** - Enemies near the impact are briefly blinded and disoriented
- **Light Trail** - Star-Shots leave a visible particle trail through the air
- **No Pickup** - Star-Shots cannot be recovered after firing

## Tips

- Snowballs are plentiful in cold biomes - never run out of ammo!
- The flashbang effect works great for crowd control
- The bow repairs itself during aurora events like all Shimmersteel gear
