# Shimmer Spear

The Shimmer Spear is a Shimmersteel-tier melee weapon that uses the new spear combat system: a long-reach piercing stab weapon that rewards charged strikes.

## Crafting

The Shimmer Spear is created by upgrading an **Iron Spear** at a Smithing Table using a **Shimmersteel Upgrade Template** and a [Shimmersteel Ingot](ingots.md).

## Abilities

### Spear Combat
- Extended reach for striking enemies from further away
- Piercing damage (hits behind shields differently from a sword)
- Right-click to charge a stab, then release for a committed strike
- Minimum attack charge of 100% — full cooldown attacks hit hardest

### Frostbite on Hit
- Every landing strike applies Frostbite to the target
- Frostbite slows, damages over time, and stacks the longer a fight goes

### Aurora Enhancement
- During aurora events, each stab deals additional damage
- Aurora-empowered Frostbite lasts twice as long at a higher strength
- Aurora particles trail from impact hits

## Tips

- Use the reach to poke through crowds and fences without exposing yourself
- Time a full-charge stab when an enemy closes in — partial swings hit softer
- Combine with [Shimmerweave Skates](../shimmerweave/skates.md) for kiting on ice
- An aurora overhead is a great window to commit to tougher fights
