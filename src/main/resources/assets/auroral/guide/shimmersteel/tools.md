# Shimmersteel Tools

Shimmersteel tools are slightly better than iron with high enchantability. Each tool also has a unique special ability!

## Pickaxe
- **Fortune III for gems** - Automatically applies Fortune III when mining diamond, emerald, lapis, and other gem ores
- Regular fortune chance for other blocks

## Axe
- **Oxidation Forcing** - Right-click any copper block to instantly advance its oxidation state
- Great for quickly aging copper builds

## Shovel
- **Inherent Silk Touch** - Always harvests blocks as if you had Silk Touch
- Perfect for collecting snow layers, grass blocks, and more

## Sword
- **Slowness Effect** - Each hit applies Slowness I to the target
- **Execute** - Deals massive bonus damage to enemies below 15% health
- **Snow Mark** - Places a snow layer where executed enemies fall

## Hoe
- **Inherent Silk Touch** - Always harvests crops and blocks as if you had Silk Touch
- **Shimmer Soil Creation** - Right-click snow blocks, powder snow, dirt, grass, or snow layers to create Shimmer Soil
- **Farmland Upgrade** - Right-click regular farmland to upgrade it to Shimmer Soil (preserves moisture)
- Perfect for starting farms in frozen biomes without needing to find dirt

### Shimmer Soil
This magical farmland accelerates crop growth:
- **3x faster** growth at night
- **5x faster** growth during aurora events
- Emits a faint glow (light level 2)
- Hydrated by water OR [Shimmering Ice](../flora/shimmering_ice.md) within 4 blocks
- Won't turn back to dirt while crops are planted
- Accepts any vanilla or modded crop seed, just like regular farmland

**Tip:** Pair with Shimmering Ice for glowing underground farms that never need water!

## Self-Repair

All Shimmersteel tools automatically repair themselves when held during an aurora event!
