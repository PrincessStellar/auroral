# The Glacial Basin

The Glacial Basin is the primary workstation for Aurorabound. This magical vessel collects Liquid Aura during aurora events and uses it to transform materials.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:glacial_basin" />

## How It Works

1. **Place the basin** outside with clear sky above
2. **Wait for an aurora** event to begin
3. **The basin fills** with glowing Liquid Aura
4. **Right-click with items** to transform them

## Transformations

The Glacial Basin can perform the following infusions when filled with Liquid Aura:

| Input | Output | Aura Cost |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| Unrefined Shimmersteel | Shimmersteel Ingot | 3 (Full) |
| Woven Leather | Shimmerweave Fabric | 1 |
| Frozen Petals | Aurora Shard | 1 |
| Ice | Shimmering Ice | 1 |

**Note:** To create a Shimmersteel Ingot, the basin must be completely full. The infusion consumes all Liquid Aura.

## Tips

- Keep multiple basins placed to collect more aura during each event
- The basin must have a clear view of the sky
- Aurora events last the entire night, giving you plenty of time to collect
