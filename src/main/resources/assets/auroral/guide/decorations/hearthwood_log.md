# Hearthwood Log

The Hearthwood Log is a magical log block that burns with an enchanted flame, providing warmth and protection from the cold.

## Obtaining

Hearthwood Logs can be found naturally in cold biomes, particularly in:
- Snowy Taiga villages
- Frozen structures
- Occasionally near Igloo basements

## Lighting the Hearthwood

Right-click the Hearthwood Log with **Flint and Steel** to ignite it. Once lit, the log will burn with a warm, magical glow.

## Properties

### Extended Burn Time
A lit Hearthwood Log burns for **7 in-game days** of chunk-loaded time (168,000 ticks, roughly 2 hours 20 minutes of continuous play while the chunk is loaded). The timer only ticks down when the chunk is active, so logs placed in rarely-visited chunks last much longer in real time. Burn time is configurable via the server config.

### Frostbite Immunity
Players within a **16-block radius** of a lit Hearthwood Log receive immunity to the Frostbite effect. This makes it invaluable for surviving harsh winter conditions.

### Phantom Ward
Any Phantom that strays into the Hearthwood Log's 16-block area of effect is instantly set ablaze. Additionally, Phantoms will refuse to target any player currently under the Frostbite Immunity effect, so a ring of lit Hearthwoods keeps the night skies clear over your base.

### Aurora Catalyst
A lit Hearthwood Log within 32 blocks of a player in a cold biome adds **+15% aurora chance** at nightfall. The bonus stacks up to three logs for a total of +45%, pushing the nightly aurora chance to about 78% on default settings. Additional logs beyond the third do not stack.

### Villager Appreciation
Villagers near a lit Hearthwood Log feel more comfortable and may offer better trading prices.

## Tips

- Place Hearthwood Logs around your cold biome base for permanent frostbite protection
- The warm glow provides excellent ambient lighting
- Multiple Hearthwood Logs stack their area of effect
