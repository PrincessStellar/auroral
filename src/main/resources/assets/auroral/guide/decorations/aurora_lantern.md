# Aurora Lantern

The Aurora Lantern is a decorative light source that captures the ethereal glow of the northern lights.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:aurora_lantern" />

## Properties

- **Light Level 15** - Provides maximum brightness
- **Color Shifting** - The lantern gently shifts through aurora colors (green, blue, pink)
- **Placeable** - Can be placed on floors, walls, or hung from ceilings
- **Waterproof** - Works underwater without extinguishing

## Darkness Immunity

When held in your **main hand or off-hand**, the Aurora Lantern provides immunity to the Darkness effect. The lantern's ethereal glow dispels magical darkness, making it invaluable when exploring Deep Dark biomes or fighting the Warden.

## Uses

Aurora Lanterns are perfect for:
- Illuminating cold biome builds
- Creating an enchanted atmosphere
- Marking paths through snowy terrain
- Decorating Shimmerweave-themed areas
