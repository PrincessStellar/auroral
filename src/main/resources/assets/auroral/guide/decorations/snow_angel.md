# Snow Angels

A playful decoration that leaves your mark in the snowy landscape.

## Creating a Snow Angel

To make a snow angel, **sneak and right-click** on any snow surface with **both hands empty**. Your character will leave an imprint in the snow, creating a decorative snow angel pattern.

A snow angel is also marked as a *replaceable* block. You can place a new block directly into its space without breaking it first, the same way you would over tall grass or a snow layer.

Snow angels can be created on:
- Snow layers
- Snow blocks
- Powder snow

## Fading Away

Snow angels are temporary by nature. Like footprints in fresh snow, they will **fade away after 5 minutes** if left alone.

## Preserving Snow Angels

If you want to keep a snow angel permanently, **right-click it with a Frozen Petal**. The petal's magical essence will crystallize the snow angel, preventing it from fading.

Once preserved, a snow angel will remain until broken.

<ItemLink id="auroral:frozen_petals">Frozen Petals</ItemLink> can be obtained by harvesting fully-grown <ItemLink id="auroral:aurora_bloom">Aurora Blooms</ItemLink> during an aurora event.
