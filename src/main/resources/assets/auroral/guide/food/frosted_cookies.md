# Frosted Cookies

Frosted Cookies are delicious treats dusted with magical Frozen Petals, giving them a sparkling shimmer.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:frosted_cookies" />

(Makes 4 Frosted Cookies)

## Properties

- **Quick Snack** - Restores a small amount of hunger
- **Shimmer Particles** - Eating produces sparkly aurora particles
- **Stackable** - Stack up to 64 for easy carrying

## Tips

- Great for quick hunger restoration on the go
- The recipe is efficient - one Frozen Petal makes 4 cookies
- Perfect companion to Hot Cocoa during cold nights
