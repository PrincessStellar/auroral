# Roasted Snowball

A snowball toasted to a gentle golden-brown, wisps of steam still curling off the top. It looks like the world's smallest marshmallow. It tastes about as substantial.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:roasted_snowball_from_smelting" />

Smelting a Snowball in a furnace works too.

## Properties

- **Minor Nutrition** - Restores a single half-drumstick of hunger
- **Always Edible** - You can nibble one even when full
- **Offensive** - Eating one marks you as a miscreant in the eyes of the Aurora

## The Warning

Eating a Roasted Snowball deeply offends the Auroral Nautili. Once you commit the act, wild Nautili **will no longer spawn near you** during aurora events. The skies grow quiet. The chimes fade.

You'll see the message *"The Magestic Nautili will no longer Soar In your skies"*, and they mean it.

## Atonement

Not all is lost. Craft a [Sugared Roasted Snowball](sugared_roasted_snowball.md) and eat it to apologize. The Nautili are forgiving creatures, and one sweet gesture is enough for them to grace your skies once more.

Alternatively, if you just want the taste without the consequences, you can also use a Roasted Snowball in cookie form by crafting a [S'nore](snore.md).
