# Hot Cocoa

A steaming mug of Hot Cocoa provides warmth and protection against the harsh cold.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:hot_cocoa" />

## Effects

When consumed, Hot Cocoa grants:
- **Frostbite Immunity** (3 minutes) - Complete protection from the Frostbite effect
- **Regeneration I** (30 seconds) - Slowly restores health
- **Hunger Restoration** - Restores moderate hunger

## Tips

- Keep a supply of Hot Cocoa when exploring cold biomes
- An empty bucket is returned when consumed (from the milk bucket used in crafting)
- Pairs well with Frosted Cookies for a cozy snack break!
