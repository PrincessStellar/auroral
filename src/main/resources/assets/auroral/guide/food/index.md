# Auroral Food & Drinks

Auroral adds several food items and drinks to help you survive and thrive in cold biomes.

## Items

- <ItemImage id="hot_cocoa" scale="0.75" /> [Hot Cocoa](hot_cocoa.md) - A warming drink with protective effects
- <ItemImage id="frosted_cookies" scale="0.75" /> [Frosted Cookies](frosted_cookies.md) - Sweet treats with a magical shimmer
- <ItemImage id="roasted_snowball" scale="0.75" /> [Roasted Snowball](roasted_snowball.md) - A toasted snowball, delicious and deeply offensive to the Nautili
- <ItemImage id="sugared_roasted_snowball" scale="0.75" /> [Sugared Roasted Snowball](sugared_roasted_snowball.md) - The apology: a sugared treat that makes amends
- <ItemImage id="snore" scale="0.75" /> [S'nore](snore.md) - 2 Cookies and a Sugared Roasted Snowball, grants Conduit Power
- <ItemImage id="glow_leek" scale="0.75" /> [Glow Leek](../flora/glow_leeks.md) - A bioluminescent vegetable
- <ItemImage id="candied_glow_leek" scale="0.75" /> [Candied Glow Leek](../flora/glow_leeks.md) - A sweetened version without the glowing side effect
