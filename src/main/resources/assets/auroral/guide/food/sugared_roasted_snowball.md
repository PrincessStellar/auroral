# Sugared Roasted Snowball

A Roasted Snowball rolled in sugar until it shines. The glint isn't magic, it's an apology in edible form.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:sugared_roasted_snowball" />

## Properties

- **Minor Nutrition** - Same small bite as the base Roasted Snowball
- **Always Edible** - Can be eaten at full hunger
- **Apology Flavoured** - Eating one makes peace with the Auroral Nautili

## The Apology

If you've eaten a [Roasted Snowball](roasted_snowball.md), the Auroral Nautili have flown off in disgust. Eating a Sugared Roasted Snowball tells them you're sorry, and they'll return on the next aurora.

You'll see the message *"The Majestic Nautili fly once again!"* confirming your good standing has been restored.

## Tips

- Craft one before you even eat the first Roasted Snowball, you may regret it otherwise
- The glint distinguishes it at a glance from its plain sibling
- No effect if the Nautili already favor you, but it still tastes sweet
