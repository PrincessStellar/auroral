# S'nore

Two Cookies and a Sugared Roasted Snowball, pressed together into a sweet little sandwich. The sugar and roasting together tame the snowball's bite. The Nautili hold no grudge against a properly plated S'nore.

## Crafting

<Recipe id="auroral:snore" />

## Properties

- **Hearty Meal** - Restores as much hunger and saturation as Cooked Beef
- **Conduit Power** (30 seconds) - Lets you breathe underwater, mine faster while submerged, and see clearly in water
- **Restful Snack** - Resets the phantom insomnia timer, as if you'd slept
- **Stacks to 16**

## Tips

- Perfect before an underwater expedition. One S'nore gets you 30 seconds of Conduit Power with none of the Heart of the Sea rigmarole
- Keep one on hand on long surface trips to ward off phantoms without needing a bed
- Unlike the raw [Roasted Snowball](roasted_snowball.md), eating a S'nore does **not** upset the Nautili. The sugar coating on the roasted snowball smooths things over
