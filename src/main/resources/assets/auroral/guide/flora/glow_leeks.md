# Glow Leeks

Glow Leeks are luminescent vegetables that thrive in the cold, grown from seeds infused with aurora energy.

## Obtaining Seeds

Glow Leek Seeds are crafted by combining **Frozen Petals** with **Wheat Seeds**:

<Recipe id="auroral:glow_leek_seeds" />

## Growing Conditions

Glow Leeks can only be planted on **Shimmering Ice** blocks. They do not require light to grow and will continue growing through the night.

## Harvesting

When fully grown, breaking a Glow Leek plant drops:
- **Glow Leek** (the vegetable)
- **Glow Leek Seeds** (for replanting)

## Eating Glow Leeks

Consuming a Glow Leek grants:
- **Night Vision** - See clearly in the dark
- **Glowing** - You become visible to others
