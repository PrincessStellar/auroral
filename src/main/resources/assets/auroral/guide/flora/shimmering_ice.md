# Shimmering Ice

Shimmering Ice is a magical form of ice infused with aurora energy. Unlike regular ice, it glows softly and never melts.

## Obtaining

Shimmering Ice can be created by:
- Infusing regular ice in a [Glacial Basin](../shimmersteel/glacial_basin.md) with Liquid Aura
- Found naturally in aurora-touched frozen biomes (rare)

## Properties

### Never Melts
Shimmering Ice is completely resistant to heat and light. It will never melt, making it perfect for decorative builds or functional ice paths.

### Light Emission
Emits a soft glow (light level 8), enough to prevent mob spawning while maintaining ambiance.

### Hydration
Shimmering Ice hydrates farmland and **Shimmer Soil** within a 4-block radius, just like water. Combined with its glow, this makes it excellent for underground farms that don't require water channels.

### Shimmer Soil Synergy
When paired with Shimmer Soil (created with a [Shimmersteel Hoe](../shimmersteel/tools.md)), you get the ultimate farming setup:
- Shimmering Ice provides hydration AND light
- Shimmer Soil provides accelerated growth (3x at night, 5x during aurora)
- No need for water - perfect for frozen biomes!

### Crop Growing Surface
**Glow Leeks** can only be planted on Shimmering Ice! The magical properties of the ice are essential for growing these bioluminescent vegetables.

## Uses

- Hydrating Shimmer Soil farms without water
- Growing Glow Leeks (exclusive planting surface)
- Creating permanent ice paths that never melt
- Decorative builds with soft lighting (light level 8)
- Underground farms with built-in lighting
