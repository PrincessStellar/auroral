# Ender Blooms

Ender Blooms are the ender-touched cousins of Aurora Blooms: purple, restless, and far more stubborn.

## Creation

Ender Blooms cannot spawn naturally. To create one:

1. Find or grow an **Aurora Bloom** during an aurora event.
2. Right-click the bloom while holding an **Ender Pearl**.
3. The bloom transforms into an Ender Bloom at the same growth stage. The pearl is consumed.

## Persistence

Unlike Aurora Blooms, Ender Blooms **persist forever**. They do not wilt at sunrise and remain when the aurora ends.

## Growth Requirements

An Ender Bloom will only progress through its growth stages when planted on one of:

- **Shimmer Soil**
- **End Stone**

On any other supporting block it will survive indefinitely but stay at whatever stage it was placed. Bonemeal is also ineffective unless planted on the blocks above.

## Harvesting

Breaking an Ender Bloom at **any** stage drops a single Ender Bloom item (which always replants as stage 0).

When broken at stage 3, it **additionally** drops:

- One **Aurora Ender Shard**
- A **5% chance** for an additional Ender Bloom
