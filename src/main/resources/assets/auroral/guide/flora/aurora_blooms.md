# Aurora Blooms

Aurora Blooms are magical flowers that spontaneously appear on snow during aurora events.

## Finding Aurora Blooms

During an aurora event, Aurora Blooms will naturally spawn on cold-biome surfaces. They grow through four stages before becoming fully mature. Any blooms left behind when the aurora ends wilt away at sunrise.

Valid surfaces for spawning and survival:

- Snow layers (provided the block beneath is itself a valid snowy surface, so blooms aren't left stranded on dirt or stone if the snow melts)
- Snow blocks
- Powder snow (see below)
- Ice, Packed Ice, and Blue Ice (frozen ocean surfaces work)
- Shimmering Ice

### Snow-Logged Blooms

When a bloom is placed on a **snow layer** or appears in **powder snow**, whether by the mod during an aurora or by you planting it yourself, it is *snow-logged*. The snow is remembered by the bloom, and breaking or wilting the bloom restores the same snow type in place (a snow layer comes back as a snow layer, powder snow comes back as powder snow). Buried blooms emit a faint trickle of snowflake particles above them so they can still be located if more snow piles on top.

## Harvesting

When fully grown (stage 3), breaking an Aurora Bloom drops:

- **Frozen Petals** (1-4 with Fortune)
- The **living Aurora Bloom** itself, so you can replant it
- A **15% chance** for an additional living Aurora Bloom on top of that

Breaking an immature bloom yields nothing, so patience is rewarded!

## Preserved Blooms (Silk Touch)

Using a **Silk Touch** tool on a fully grown Aurora Bloom yields a **decorative Aurora Bloom** instead of Frozen Petals. The decorative variant:

- Never decays at sunrise. It remains forever
- Is always the fully grown stage 3 appearance
- Can be placed inside a **Flower Pot** for display
- Drops itself when broken (clone it to a new location anytime)

This is the only way to preserve an Aurora Bloom outside of an active aurora.

## Uses for Frozen Petals

Frozen Petals are used in several recipes:

- **Awkward Potions** - Use like Nether Wart in a brewing stand to create Awkward Potions from Water Bottles
- **Cold Brewing Stand** - Craft with Shimmersteel Ingots to create a Nether-free brewing station
- **Glow Leek Seeds** - Combine with wheat seeds to grow Glow Leeks
- **Frosted Cookies** - A cold treat with special effects
- **Aurora Shards** - Infuse in a Glacial Basin with 1 aura level to create Aurora Shards

## Ender Transformation

Right-clicking an Aurora Bloom with an **Ender Pearl** transforms it into an **Ender Bloom** at the same growth stage. The pearl is consumed in the process. See [Ender Blooms](ender_bloom.md) for details.
