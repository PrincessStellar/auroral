# Aurora Events

The Aurora is the celestial phenomenon at the heart of Aurorabound.

## When Auroras Occur

Auroras have a 33% chance to begin each night in cold biomes. When an aurora starts:
- The sky fills with colorful ribbons of light (green and pink)
- The night is never fully dark
- Magical properties activate

## Effects During an Aurora

### Liquid Aura Collection
Glacial Basins exposed to the sky will slowly fill with Liquid Aura, the magical substance needed for infusions.

### Gear Repair
Shimmersteel tools and Shimmerweave armor automatically repair themselves while equipped during an aurora.

### Creature Spawning
Auroral Nautiluses - magical flying creatures - may spawn near players during aurora events.

### Flora Growth
Aurora Blooms spontaneously appear on snow blocks, ready to be harvested for Frozen Petals.

## Aurora Catalyst

The Hearthwood Log acts as an Aurora Catalyst. Each lit Hearthwood Log within 32 blocks of a player in a cold biome adds +15% aurora chance at nightfall, stacking up to three logs (+45%) for a nightly chance of about 78% on default settings.

The catalyst is a strong boost, not a guarantee. For a near-certain aurora, keep several lit logs near where you sleep and make sure you are in a cold biome when night begins.
