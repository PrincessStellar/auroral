# Creatures of the Aurora

The aurora brings forth magical beings from the celestial realms.

## Auroral Nautilus

The Auroral Nautilus is a peaceful floating creature that only appears during aurora events. These ethereal beings drift through the sky, their shells glowing with auroral light.

### Wild Behavior
- Spawns only during active auroras
- Floats gently through the air, drifting in circular patterns
- Peaceful and non-hostile
- Comes in three sizes
- Fades away gracefully 30 seconds after the aurora ends

### Attracting Nautili
Wild Auroral Nautili are curious creatures and will be attracted to players holding Aurora Shards. Hold a shard in your hand and nearby nautili will slowly drift toward you, making them easier to tame.

### Taming
Feed a wild Auroral Nautilus an Aurora Shard to attempt taming (33% success chance per shard). When successfully tamed:
- The nautilus will orbit slowly around you
- It will no longer despawn when the aurora ends
- Heart particles appear to indicate successful taming

### Commanding Your Nautilus
Once tamed, you can command your Auroral Nautilus:
- **Shift + Right-click**: Toggle between following and staying
  - When staying, the nautilus hovers in place
  - When following, it slowly orbits around you

### Riding
Tamed Auroral Nautili can be ridden without a saddle:
1. Right-click to mount (owner only, nautilus must not be in "stay" mode)
2. Control flight with movement keys and looking up/down
3. Hold Space to charge, release for a speed boost (up to 3x speed when fully charged)

### Nautilus Armor
Auroral Nautili can wear vanilla nautilus armor (Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond, and Netherite variants). To equip a piece, right-click the tamed nautilus while holding the armor in your main hand. It slots into the body slot.

To remove it, right-click the nautilus with **shears** (without sneaking). The armor pops off and drops on the ground; the shears take 1 durability damage.

### Drops
When an Auroral Nautilus fades away (or is slain), it may drop:
- Aurora Shards (1-2, player kill only)
- Nautilus Shells (15% chance, player kill only)

### Offending the Nautili
Eating a [Roasted Snowball](../food/roasted_snowball.md) offends the Aurora. While you carry that offense, wild Auroral Nautili **will not spawn near you** during aurora events. Only tamed nautili remain. To atone, craft and eat a [Sugared Roasted Snowball](../food/sugared_roasted_snowball.md) and the skies will once again grace you with their chimes.

## Auroral Snowlette

The Auroral Snowlette is a small, friendly companion created by right-clicking a Snow Golem with an Aurora Shard.

### Behavior
- Follows the player like a pet
- About 1/4 the size of a normal Snow Golem
- Can be healed by throwing snowballs at it
- Emits gentle aurora sparkles

These adorable creatures make loyal companions for adventures in cold biomes!
