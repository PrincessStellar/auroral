# Shimmerweave Goggles

The Shimmerweave Goggles are enchanted eyewear that reveal hidden threats in the darkness.

## Crafting

Upgrade Iron Helmet at a Smithing Table using a Shimmersteel Upgrade Template and Shimmerweave Fabric.

<RecipeFor id="auroral:shimmerweave_goggles" />

## Abilities

### Hostile Detection
- **Glowing Effect** - All hostile mobs within 32 blocks glow, visible through walls
- Perfect for cave exploration and night raids
- Works in complete darkness

## Tips

- Combine with Night Vision potions or [Glow-Leeks](../flora/glow_leeks.md) for full visibility
- Essential for exploring dark caves safely
- The 32-block radius covers most combat situations
