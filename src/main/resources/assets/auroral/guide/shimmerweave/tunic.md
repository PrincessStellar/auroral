# Shimmerweave Tunic

The Shimmerweave Tunic is a magical chestpiece that protects the wearer from fire and flames.

## Crafting

Upgrade Iron Chestplate at a Smithing Table using a Shimmersteel Upgrade Template and Shimmerweave Fabric.

<RecipeFor id="auroral:shimmerweave_tunic" />

## Abilities

### Auto-Extinguish
- **Instant Fire Protection** - Automatically extinguishes fire when you catch on fire
- No fire damage taken while wearing the tunic
- Works against all fire sources: lava, fire blocks, blaze attacks

### Cooling Aura
The aurora-infused fabric naturally repels heat, making it invaluable for:
- Nether exploration
- Fighting Blazes
- Working near lava

## Tips

- Essential for safe Nether travel
- Pairs well with [Shimmerweave Skates](skates.md) which convert lava to obsidian
- The fire protection is passive - no activation needed
