# Shimmerweave Leggings

The Shimmerweave Leggings grant enhanced mobility across snow and soul sand.

## Crafting

Upgrade Iron Leggings at a Smithing Table using a Shimmersteel Upgrade Template and Shimmerweave Fabric.

<RecipeFor id="auroral:shimmerweave_leggings" />

## Abilities

### Snow Speed
- **Speed Boost** - Move faster while walking on snow blocks
- No slowdown from snow layers
- Perfect for traversing snowy biomes

### Soul Speed
- **Soul Sand Mastery** - Built-in Soul Speed effect on soul sand and soul soil
- Does not damage the leggings (unlike the enchantment)
- Traverse the Nether's soul sand valleys with ease

## Tips

- Combine with [Shimmerweave Skates](skates.md) for ultimate mobility
- The soul speed effect stacks with the Soul Speed enchantment
- Great for both Overworld snow biomes and Nether soul sand valleys
