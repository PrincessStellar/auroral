# Shimmerweave Skates

The Shimmerweave Skates are the most versatile piece of Shimmerweave armor, granting mastery over ice and cold terrain.

## Abilities

### Speed on Ice and Snow
- **Speed boost** while standing on ice or snow blocks
- **Extra speed** on packed ice and blue ice

### Ice Skating Momentum
- Build up momentum on ice like a boat!
- Keep moving to accelerate, release keys to coast
- Blue ice provides the smoothest skating surface
- Momentum carries even when not pressing movement keys

### Frost Walker Enhancement
- Creates frosted ice when walking on water
- Also converts **lava to obsidian** on contact!

### Fall Immunity
- Take no fall damage when landing on ice or obsidian
- Perfect for skating down from heights

## Synergy

The Shimmerweave Skates pair excellently with:
- **Shimmerweave Leggings** - Additional speed on snow and soul sand
- **Shimmersteel Shovel** - Quickly create snow paths with Silk Touch
- The lava-to-obsidian conversion makes traversing the Nether safer!
