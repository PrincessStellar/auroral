# Introduction to Auroral

Auroral transforms cold biomes from barren wastes into hubs of high-tier progression. By harnessing the power of the Aurora Borealis, you can create magical items, enhanced tools, and powerful armor.

## Key Concepts

### The Aurora

The Aurora is a celestial event that occurs randomly during nights in cold biomes. When the aurora is active, the sky fills with shimmering ribbons of green and pink light. This is when the magic happens.

During an aurora:
- **Liquid Aura** can be collected in Glacial Basins
- **Shimmersteel gear** repairs itself automatically
- **Auroral Nautiluses** may spawn in the skies
- **Aurora Blooms** appear on snow blocks

### Cold Biomes

Auroral's features are designed for cold biomes such as:
- Snowy Plains
- Ice Spikes
- Frozen Ocean
- Frozen Peaks
- Grove
- Snowy Taiga

### Progression Path

1. Wait for an Aurora event in a cold biome
2. Harvest **Frozen Petals** from naturally spawning Aurora Blooms
3. Craft a Glacial Basin and Cold Brewing Stand
4. Collect Liquid Aura during auroras to infuse iron into Shimmersteel
5. Craft powerful tools and armor
