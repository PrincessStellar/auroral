# Your First Steps

Ready to begin your journey into Auroral? Here's how to get started.

## Gathering Frozen Petals

Frozen Petals are the key to unlocking Auroral's content. You can find them:
- Harvested from **Aurora Blooms** that spawn naturally on snow during aurora events
- Dropped by Auroral Nautiluses during aurora events

## Crafting the Glacial Basin

The Glacial Basin is your primary workstation. Craft it with:

<Recipe id="auroral:glacial_basin" />

Place the basin outside during an aurora event to fill it with Liquid Aura.

## Your First Shimmersteel

Once your Glacial Basin has collected Liquid Aura:

1. Right-click the basin while holding an Iron Ingot
2. The iron will transform into Shimmersteel
3. Use Shimmersteel to craft enhanced tools and weapons

If you want to explore alchemy without venturing to the Nether:

### Frozen Petals as Brewing Ingredient

Frozen Petals harvested from Aurora Blooms can be used to brew **Awkward Potions** from Water Bottles, just like Nether Wart. This provides a complete Nether-free path to potion brewing!
