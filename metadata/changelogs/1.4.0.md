# Auroral 1.4.0

## Added

- **Roasted Snowball** - A new edible item smelted from a snowball (furnace or campfire). Provides minor nutrition. Eating one offends the Auroral Nautili, and wild nautili will no longer spawn near you during aurora events until you atone
- **Sugared Roasted Snowball** - Crafted from a Roasted Snowball and sugar. Carries a visible enchantment glint. Eating one makes peace with the Nautili so they return to your skies
- **S'nore** - A shapeless craft of 2 Cookies and a Sugared Roasted Snowball. Restores as much hunger and saturation as Cooked Beef, grants Conduit Power for 30 seconds, and resets the phantom insomnia timer as if you'd slept. Stacks to 16. Currently uses the vanilla cookie texture as a placeholder
- Hover tooltips on Roasted Snowball and Sugared Roasted Snowball explaining their effect on the Auroral Nautili
- GuideMe entries for all three new foods, plus a note on the Auroral Nautilus page about how offending the Nautili affects wild spawns
- Shimmersteel Sword execute now plays distinctive feedback when it triggers: an enchant, crit, and end-rod particle burst at the target, with a deep crit hit sound layered with a high amethyst chime
- Shimmerweave Goggles now have a translucent tinted visor over the eye area. The opaque side bars and rim stay, but you can see the wearer's face and eyes through the goggles
- Auroral Nautili now support vanilla Nautilus Armor (Copper, Iron, Golden, Diamond, Netherite). Right-click a tamed nautilus with a piece of nautilus armor to equip it, and shear the nautilus with shears to remove the armor. Armor now renders correctly on the entity via a custom shell overlay

## Changed

- Tamed Auroral Nautili now orbit closer (radius 3.0/height 2.0 to 2.0/1.0) so they stay within survival interact reach. You can now actually right-click to sit, mount, or equip them without waiting for a lucky pass
- Wild Auroral Nautili now orbit a player holding an Aurora Shard instead of hovering directly overhead, keeping themselves in reach for taming clicks

## Fixed

- Shimmersteel Bow damage now scales correctly with the Power enchantment. The Star-Shot projectile was missing from the `#minecraft:arrows` entity tag, which is required for the vanilla Power enchantment to apply. A fully-drawn Power V Shimmersteel Bow now lands about 8.5 hearts on an unarmored target instead of the previous 3.5
- Shimmersteel Sword execute now also honors the weapon item carried by the damage source (not just the attacker's main hand) and treats targets exactly at the execute threshold as eligible
- Shimmersteel Ingot can now actually be used as an armor trim material. Smithing tables accept it as the addition slot for armor trim recipes, and it maps to the `auroral:shimmersteel` trim material
