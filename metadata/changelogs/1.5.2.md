# Auroral 1.5.2

## Added

- Tooltips on every Auroral weapon, armor piece, and functional block explaining what they do. Shimmersteel tools, Shimmerweave armor, the Shimmer Spear, Hearthwood Log, Glacial Basin, Cold Brewing Stand, Shimmering Ice, Shimmer Soil, Snow Angels, Aurora Blooms, Ender Blooms, Glow-Leeks, Aurora Lanterns, Frozen Petals, and Aurora Ender Shards all call out their abilities so you don't have to check the guide mid-fight.

## Changed

- **Shimmer Spear** is now a proper stabbing spear instead of a throwable trident. It uses the 26.1 spear combat style: long reach, piercing stab, hold right-click to charge and release for a committed strike. Every hit still applies Frostbite, and during an aurora it deals bonus damage and inflicts a longer, stronger Frostbite.

## Fixed

- Aurora Lanterns now drop themselves when broken instead of disappearing.
- Glow Leeks now drop a leek plus bonus seeds when harvested at full maturity, like wheat. Reap mod's right-click harvest works correctly too.
- Aurora Blooms growing on snow now drop Frozen Petals and the decorative variant when broken. The snow restore was happening before the drops rolled and was eating them.
- Young Aurora Blooms (stages 0-2) are no longer hidden inside a single snow layer when planted on snow.
- The Aurora Bloom item tooltip no longer shows the raw translation key and now says "Aurora Bloom" like it should.
- Hearthwood Logs can be relit with flint and steel after they burn out, matching the guide.
- Shimmersteel Axes strip logs, scrape copper, and remove wax again — not only force-oxidize copper.
- Shimmersteel Shovels can create dirt paths and douse campfires again.
- The Shimmersteel Upgrade Smithing Template no longer shows the magenta-and-black "missing texture" checkerboard in empty slots; the ice-themed icons for all equipment types display correctly. Hoe and Spear slot icons are now included in the rotation since both are upgrade targets. (fixes issue #6)
- Broken guide links on the Shimmer Spear page no longer throw "Page does not exist" errors. (fixes issue #5)
- Shimmering Ice now hydrates vanilla farmland within 4 blocks, not just Shimmer Soil, matching the guide. Works for Shimmering Ice placed before this update too. (fixes issue #4)
- The `show_aurora_particles` and `play_aurora_ambient_sound` client config toggles now actually do something. Previously both were defined but never read, so toggling them had no effect. (fixes issue #1)

## Docs

- Guide index pages now show a small item icon next to each entry so the table of contents is easier to scan.
- Hearthwood Log guide now accurately describes the Aurora Catalyst boost: +15% aurora chance per lit log nearby, stacking up to three logs for +45%. The two guide pages previously contradicted each other (one said "guaranteed", the other said "slight").
- Hearthwood Log burn time corrected from "7 real-world days" to "7 in-game days of play time" (roughly two and a half hours while the area is loaded). The timer only counts down while the chunk is active.
