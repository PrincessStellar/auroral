# Auroral 1.5.2

## Changed

- **Shimmer Spear** now uses the vanilla 26.1 spear combat system: long-reach piercing stab with charged right-click, instead of the previous throwable trident-style behavior. Frostbite is now applied on every stab hit, and aurora events add bonus damage plus extended, stronger Frostbite.

## Fixed

- Aurora Lantern now drops itself when broken (missing loot table).
- Glow Leek now drops the leek item plus bonus seeds at full maturity (wheat-style), fixing Reap mod harvest returning nothing.
- Aurora Bloom drops the decorative variant / Frozen Petals again when broken in the snow-logged state (snow restore was being processed before drops, cancelling them).
- Aurora Blooms at stages 0 to 2 are now visible above a single snow layer (offset cross model when snow-logged).
- Aurora Bloom item tooltip now shows the correct translation instead of the raw `item.auroral.aurora_bloom` key.
- Hearthwood Log can be relit with flint and steel after burning out, as described in the guide.
- Shimmersteel Axe strips logs and wax-offs / scrapes copper in addition to its forced-oxidation right-click (now extends AxeItem).
- Shimmersteel Shovel creates dirt paths and douses campfires (now extends ShovelItem).
