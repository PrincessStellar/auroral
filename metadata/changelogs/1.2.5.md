# Auroral 1.2.5

## Fixed

- Fixed Shimmer Soil rejecting vanilla crop seeds
- Added the proper vanilla tool tags to weapons and tools

## Tweaks

- A fully grown Aurora Bloom now also drops the living bloom itself in addition to Frozen Petals, with a 15% chance for one bonus bloom. broken blooms can be replanted and slowly propagated
- A fully grown Ender Bloom has a 5% chance to drop a bonus Ender Bloom in addition to its Aurora Ender Shard
- Lit Hearthwood Logs now ignite any Phantom that enters their 16-block area of effect, and Phantoms refuse to target players who are in the protective area
