# Auroral 1.2.1

## Ported to Minecraft 26.1.2

- Updated to Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge 26.1.2.7-beta
- Updated JEI to 29.5.0.26 for 26.1.2

## New Content

### Decorative Aurora Bloom

- Silk-touching a fully grown Aurora Bloom now yields a **decorative** variant instead of Frozen Petals
- The decorative bloom never decays at sunrise, always displays as fully grown, and can be placed inside flower pots
- Breaking the decorative variant simply drops itself, allowing it to be relocated freely

### Ender Bloom

- Right-click an Aurora Bloom with an **Ender Pearl** to transform it into an **Ender Bloom** at the same growth stage (pearl is consumed)
- Ender Blooms persist forever — they do not wilt at sunrise like Aurora Blooms
- Ender Blooms only progress through growth stages when planted on **Shimmer Soil** or **End Stone** (bonemeal follows the same rule)
- Breaking an Ender Bloom at any stage drops an Ender Bloom item; at stage 3 it additionally drops an **Aurora Ender Shard**
- 2 Aurora Ender Shards can be shapeless-crafted back into an Ender Pearl

### Guide Updates

- Added a new Ender Blooms guide page under Flora
- Updated the Aurora Blooms guide with silk-touch preservation and ender transformation sections
